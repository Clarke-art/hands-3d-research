# Third-party sources and exceptions

* PianoPlayer fixed core: Marco Musy, MIT. See [original license](third_party/pianoplayer_core/LICENSE) and [pinned provenance and local changes](third_party/pianoplayer_core/PROVENANCE.md). Original notice remains intact.
* Blender Human Base Meshes v1.4.1: Blender Studio and community contributions, CC0 source evidence. See [evidence](examples/minimal/LICENSE_EVIDENCE.md). The realistic hand is credited to Dan Ulrich in retained evidence. This is source ancestry, not an assertion that all later rig/model contributions are CC0 or owned by Clarke-art. Embedded notices remain unchanged.
* Mutopia editions: MIDI files retain the saved public-domain edition and typesetter provenance. See [retained source evidence](examples/minimal/SOURCE_AND_LICENSE_R1.md) and the per-file manifest. Musette BWV Anh.126's retained edition credits Allen Garvin; no new historical composer adjudication is made.
* PianoVAM raw/derived external motion and reference cohort: excluded. Current CC BY-NC versus historical CC BY-NC-SA is unresolved at the historical version/derivative boundary. See [version binding](docs/provenance/DATASET_LICENSE_BINDING.md). No MIT/CC BY relicensing is applied.
* Lumikey weights/training data and unqualified reference material: excluded, retained as versions, original SHA and references. npm MIT metadata alone is not a blanket grant over weights/training content. Original adapters and research conclusions remain in the selected scope.
* Python/npm libraries are independently obtained under their upstream terms; do not redistribute installed dependency trees. See [rebuild](environment/REBUILD.md) and [reference index](docs/provenance/THIRD_PARTY_REFERENCE_INDEX.csv).

The per-file R1 manifest is retained unchanged. R2_LICENSE_SCOPE.csv records the proposed layering and unclosed approval gate. A reference or publicly downloadable URL is not redistribution permission.
